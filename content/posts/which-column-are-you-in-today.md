There is a line I keep coming back to:

> There is no changing. There is only changed, or hasn't changed.

Thinking about becoming more patient is not patience. Reading about forgiveness is not forgiving. Resolving to be more honest tomorrow is not honesty. The actual test of whether you've changed is boring and specific: are you behaving differently or not?

Which means you can't measure the person you're becoming by what you believe about yourself. You can only measure it by which **column** your choices actually land in, on a given day, in a given moment.

Here are the columns.

---

## The eight pairs

Each row is a pair of columns. Mature on the left, immature on the right. You are not permanently in either column. You drift toward one or the other, decision by decision.

```
MATURE                              IMMATURE
---------------------------------   ---------------------------------
Responsible                         Irresponsible
Humility & Honesty                  Arrogance & Pride
Slow to Anger                       Easily Angered
Promotes Unity                      Promotes Disunity
Desire to Grow                      Desire to Feel Happy & Gratified
Faith for the Future                Fear of the Future
Decisive & Progress Driven          Driven & Double-Minded
Governed by Facts                   Governed by Feelings
```

Walk down the rows and ask yourself, honestly, which column each decision today actually landed in. Not which one you *want* to be in. Which one you *were* in.

---

## Row 1: Responsibility

The question: when something didn't go well today, did I look at my part in it, or did I look at someone else's?

**Mature column:** "What did I contribute to this? What can I change next time?"
**Immature column:** "This happened because of them, the system, my mood, bad luck, my past."

Blame looks backward and outward. Responsibility looks forward and inward. One of them produces change. The other produces grievances.

---

## Row 2: Humility & Honesty

The question: when I'm wrong, do I notice? And when I notice, do I say so?

**Mature column:** "I was wrong about that. Here's what I missed."
**Immature column:** "I was basically right. You just took it the wrong way."

Arrogance is not loudness. It's the quiet assumption that your view of the situation is the view of the situation. Humility is the willingness to be shown you were seeing it from one angle.

---

## Row 3: Slow to Anger

The question: how fast did I go from a trigger to a reaction today?

**Mature column:** A pause. A breath. A second to notice before responding.
**Immature column:** Instant. Reflexive. No gap at all.

The practice is the gap. The gap is what [STOPP](/posts/five-letters-before-you-react/) is for.

---

## Row 4: Promotes Unity

The question: in the conversations I had today, did I build connection or quietly chip at it?

**Mature column:** Tried to understand what the other person was actually saying. Looked for common ground even in disagreement.
**Immature column:** Needed to be right. Scored a point. Dismissed someone. Walked away rather than stay in it.

Unity takes effort. Disunity is the default. If you want connection, you have to actively fight for it. Nobody drifts into deep relationships.

---

## Row 5: Desire to Grow vs Desire to Feel Happy

This is the sneakiest row.

**Mature column:** Chose a thing today that was uncomfortable but useful. Said the hard thing. Stayed with the difficult conversation. Did the work.
**Immature column:** Reached for whatever felt good *right now*. The snack. The scroll. The avoidance. The thing that soothed instead of grew.

Growing hurts a little. Comfort doesn't. The honest signal that you're drifting into the right column is a steady, mild, tolerable discomfort. The signal that you're in the wrong one is that everything feels fine and nothing is actually changing.

---

## Row 6: Faith for the Future

**Faith here is not a religious word.** It's an attitude. The opposite of fear-of-the-future isn't optimism or belief. It's just the posture of walking forward *as if* tomorrow is worth walking toward.

**Mature column:** Acted today in a way that bet on a good future existing. Made a plan. Took a step. Invested in something that pays off later.
**Immature column:** Reacted today as if the future had already gone badly. Hoarded. Hedged. Waited. Didn't start.

Fear of the future doesn't look like fear. It usually looks like paralysis dressed up as caution.

---

## Row 7: Decisive & Progress Driven

The question: did I decide something today, or did I keep thinking about deciding?

**Mature column:** Made a call. Even a small one. Moved.
**Immature column:** Stayed in the maybe. Weighed the same thing I weighed yesterday. Postponed.

The distinction matters: *driven* is in the immature column too, when it means busy-but-double-minded. Running fast in no direction is not progress. **Decisiveness is the choice to pick a direction and go.** Being wrong about the direction is survivable. Not picking isn't.

---

## Row 8: Governed by Facts vs Governed by Feelings

The question: when I decided something today, did I decide because of what was actually true, or because of what I was feeling at the time?

**Mature column:** Checked. Asked. Looked at the actual evidence. Allowed the feeling to be present without letting it drive.
**Immature column:** Felt a thing and acted on it. Interpreted the feeling as data about reality.

Feelings are real. They are also almost never a reliable source of information about external facts. Letting a feeling have a voice is wise. Letting a feeling steer the car is how most bad decisions happen.

---

## How to actually use this

Not as a scorecard. As a daily look.

```
Today, in each row, which column did I actually land in?

Row 1 (Responsibility):          Mature  /  Immature
Row 2 (Humility):                Mature  /  Immature
Row 3 (Slow to Anger):           Mature  /  Immature
Row 4 (Unity):                   Mature  /  Immature
Row 5 (Growth vs Gratification): Mature  /  Immature
Row 6 (Faith for the Future):    Mature  /  Immature
Row 7 (Decisive):                Mature  /  Immature
Row 8 (Facts vs Feelings):       Mature  /  Immature
```

Honest answers. Not the person you want to be. The person you *were* today.

The exercise is not to score high. It is to notice where you drifted into the right column honestly, and where you drifted into the left column honestly, so that tomorrow you can make one specific different choice in one specific row.

Pick the row that is most clearly in the wrong column, and make tomorrow's choice in that row differently. Just that one. **One row, one choice.** That is how the columns actually shift.

---

## The one line

If you keep nothing else from this post:

> There is no changing. There is only changed, or hasn't changed.

The columns do not move because you understood this post. They move because you made a different choice in one specific row today, and then again tomorrow, and then again next week, and then one morning you noticed that you were just living in the right column of that row and had almost forgotten you used to live in the other one. That is the only way any of this ever happens.
